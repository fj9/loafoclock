## Storing

- **Room temperature:** cut side down on a board, or in a linen bag, bread bin or paper bag. Good for 2-4 days.
- **Avoid the fridge:** it makes bread stale faster. (Only useful in very hot, humid weather.)
- **Plastic bags** soften the crust but also delay staling; use only after the first day if you prefer a soft crust.

Sourdough keeps better than yeasted bread thanks to its acidity.

## Freezing

- Slice before freezing so you can toast slices straight from the freezer.
- Wrap tightly or use freezer bags. Keeps well for up to 3 months.
- Whole loaves can be frozen and reheated.

## Reviving stale bread

- Splash the crust lightly with water and bake at about 180°C for 5-10 minutes to refresh.
- Stale bread makes excellent toast, croutons, breadcrumbs, panzanella and French toast.
