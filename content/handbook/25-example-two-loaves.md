Make a double batch, shape two loaves, and put both in the fridge together. One is baked after 16 hours and the other after 40, so you have a loaf on Day 3 and another on Day 4, 24 hours apart. The second loaf spends longer in the cold, so it is the more sour of the two. That is useful if you want fresh bread on two different days, or one loaf to give away.

It follows the [[example-overnight-levain|overnight levain timeline]] exactly, with double the amounts and one extra step: after the bulk, divide the dough in two at the pre-shape. The fridge times are 16 and 40 hours instead of shorter ones so that both bakes fall in the morning.

## The plan

This is the first loaf, with 16 hours in the fridge. It is the same plan as the overnight levain, with two loaves in it.

{{planFigure twoLoavesFirst}}

**Day 1.** Feed the starter at {{time twoLoavesFirst feed}} and go to bed. It rises overnight.

**Day 2.** The flour and water go together at {{time twoLoavesFirst autolyse}}, the starter goes in at {{time twoLoavesFirst starterIn}} and the salt, honey and oil at {{time twoLoavesFirst salt}}. After four folds the bulk runs on into the afternoon. When it is done, at {{time twoLoavesFirst preShape}}, divide the dough in half, pre-shape both pieces, rest them {{benchRest}} minutes and shape them. Both go into the fridge at {{time twoLoavesFirst fridge}}.

**Day 3.** Bake the first loaf and leave the other in the fridge. Turn the oven on at {{time twoLoavesFirst ovenOn}}, bake at {{time twoLoavesFirst bake}}, and carve at {{time twoLoavesFirst carve}}.

## The second loaf

The second loaf stays in the fridge for 40 hours, and is baked in exactly the same way, 24 hours later:

- **Oven on:** {{at twoLoavesSecond ovenOn}}
- **Bake:** {{at twoLoavesSecond bake}}
- **Carve:** {{at twoLoavesSecond carve}}

The app allows up to {{fridgeMax}} hours in the fridge, so 40 is well inside it.

Keep the second loaf covered, and check it before you bake: a dough left in the fridge this long is looser and more sour. If it has spread or feels slack, shape it again, rest it for 30 minutes and bake it a little sooner.
