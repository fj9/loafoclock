This edition of the handbook is written so that you can give it to an AI assistant such as Microsoft Copilot, and ask questions about your own bake while you are in the middle of it.

**To use it with Copilot**

- Open Microsoft Copilot (the app or the website) and start a new chat.
- Attach this PDF (the edition for an AI assistant, which you can download from the Handbook page in the app) using the **+** or paperclip button next to the message box.
- Paste the starter message below as your first message.
- Ask questions as you bake. Describe what you can see and measure. The more detail you give, the better the help.

**Starter message (copy and paste this)**

> I've attached a sourdough handbook. Please use it as your main source for everything I ask. I'm a home baker and I'm finding sourdough difficult, so please be patient, keep your answers short and give me one step at a time. Use grams and degrees Celsius. Before you suggest a fix, ask me what I did and what I can see. If the handbook doesn't cover something, tell me.

**Tips**

- If Copilot seems to forget the handbook, or the chat gets long and muddled, start a new chat, attach the PDF again and paste the starter message again.
- To make it look something up, name the section: "Using the troubleshooting table in [[troubleshooting|section 12]], why is my loaf flat?"
- If Copilot lets you attach a photo, a picture of your dough, starter jar or sliced loaf can help.
- Copilot can make mistakes. If it says something that disagrees with this handbook, ask it to check the handbook again. For safety points (hot pots, mould, raw dough) always follow the handbook.

**An example of a good question for Copilot**

> My loaf came out flat and gummy. My kitchen was about 20°C. I fed my starter at 8am with 20 g starter, 100 g flour and 100 g water and used it at 4pm. It had risen but wasn't domed. The dough was 22°C after mixing and I let it bulk for 5 hours without marking how far it rose. I shaped it, proofed it in the fridge overnight and baked it at 230°C for 40 minutes. What went wrong, and what should I change next time?

What to write down while you bake, so that you can answer the questions an assistant will ask, is in [[what-to-note|section A2]].
