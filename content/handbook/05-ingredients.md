## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Notes |
|---|---|
| Flour | Strong/bread flour (roughly 12-14% protein) gives the best structure. Add wholemeal, rye or spelt for flavour and a livelier ferment. |
| Water | Tap water is fine for almost everyone. Lukewarm, around 25-30°C, unless you are targeting a specific dough temperature. |
| Salt | About 2% of flour weight. Fine sea salt dissolves most easily. Salt flavours the bread and tightens the dough. |
| Starter | Active and recently fed (see [[starter|section 3]]). |

## Kit

**Essential**

- Digital scales (grams, 1 g resolution; 0.1 g is handy for salt)
- Large bowl or tub (a clear straight-sided tub makes it easy to judge the rise)
- Jar for the starter
- Something hot and heavy to bake in: a lidded cast-iron pot or Dutch oven works best at home
- Wire rack

**Strongly recommended**

- Bench scraper
- Banneton (proofing basket) or a bowl lined with a floured tea towel
- Sharp blade or lame for scoring
- Rubber bands or tape to mark the starting level on a jar or tub
- Thermometer (probe style, for dough, water and the baked loaf)

**Nice to have**

- Stand mixer (not required)
- Baking steel or stone
- Spray bottle
- Linen couche
- Parchment paper or a silicone sling for lifting the loaf into the pot
