## Temperatures

| Stage | Temperature |
|---|---|
| Starter | 24–27°C |
| Bulk | 24–26°C (see the bulk chart in [[process#bulk|section 6, bulk fermentation]]) |
| Fridge proof | 3–5°C ({{fridgeMin}} to {{fridgeMax}} hours) |
| Final proof, no fridge | room temperature (see [[process#final-proof-no-fridge|the final proof table]]) |
| Preheat | {{ovenOn}}°C |
| Bake, lid on | {{ovenOn}}°C |
| Bake, lid off | {{ovenLidOff}}°C |
| Done | Hollow sound from the base; 96–99°C if you use a probe |

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. Go by colour as well as the clock.

## Checklist before baking

- Starter was ripe when used
- Bulk finished: dough domed, airy, wobbly, risen by the target for your dough temperature (see [[process#bulk|the bulk table]])
- Shaped tightly, seam sealed
- Proof checked with the poke test
- Oven and pot fully preheated
- Dough scored
- Steam provided
- Bake until deep golden
- Cool for at least {{coolingLong}} minutes before cutting ({{coolingShort}} if you have no self-control)

## The one-sentence version

Feed a healthy starter, mix at the right temperature, build strength gently, judge fermentation by the dough rather than the clock, use the fridge to move the timing around your life, bake hot with steam, and let it cool.

*Happy baking.*
