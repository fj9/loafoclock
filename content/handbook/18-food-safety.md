title: Food safety notes
part: B
---

- Wash hands and equipment. The wild culture is robust, but cleanliness avoids contamination.
- **Warning:** if you see mould, or pink or orange streaks, on a starter, throw it all away.
- **Warning:** do not eat raw dough. Raw flour and raw dough can carry harmful bacteria (flour is a raw agricultural product), so wash your hands and surfaces after handling them.
- Do not seal the starter jar tightly: gas pressure can build up, and glass can crack.
- **Warning:** a lidded cast-iron pot at {{ovenOn}}°C causes serious burns. Use proper gloves and put a heat-safe surface nearby.
- Gluten: sourdough made with wheat is not gluten free, however long it ferments. Anyone with coeliac disease should follow the advice of their doctor or a coeliac charity, such as Coeliac UK or [Beyond Celiac](https://www.beyondceliac.org/gluten-free-diet/is-it-gluten-free/sourdough-bread/).
- Allergies: check any additions such as nuts, seeds and dairy.
- Honey: do not give honey, or food made with honey, to babies under 12 months. The honey is optional, so leave it out if the bread is for a baby.
- Mould on bread: if a loaf has any mould, throw the whole loaf away. Do not cut the mould off.
