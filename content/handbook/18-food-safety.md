title: Food safety notes
part: B
---

- **Wash hands and equipment.** The wild culture is robust, but cleanliness avoids contamination.
- **Mould or pink/orange streaks on a starter:** throw it away.
- **Raw flour and raw dough** can carry harmful bacteria (flour is a raw agricultural product). Do not eat raw dough, and wash hands and surfaces after handling it.
- **Don't seal the starter jar tightly:** gas pressure can build up, and glass can crack.
- **Hot equipment:** a lidded cast-iron pot at 250°C causes serious burns. Use proper gloves and put a heat-safe surface nearby.
- **Gluten:** sourdough is not gluten-free. Long fermentation may reduce gluten somewhat, but it does not make wheat bread safe for people with coeliac disease.
- **Allergies:** check any additions like nuts, seeds and dairy.
