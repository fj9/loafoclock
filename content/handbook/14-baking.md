## Why steam matters

Steam keeps the crust soft and stretchy for the first part of baking so the loaf can expand fully ("oven spring"), and later helps produce a thin, shiny, crisp crust.

## Dutch oven / lidded pot method (easiest) {#dutch-oven steps=ovenOn,bake}

- Put the empty pot with its lid in the oven and heat it to **{{ovenOn}}°C**. Allow {{preheat}} minutes.
- Lift the shaped dough on parchment or a sling (straight from the fridge is fine).
- Score the top with a sharp blade at a shallow angle, about 0.5-1 cm deep.
- Carefully lower the dough into the hot pot, add a few ice cubes for steam (they spit, so keep your face back), then put the lid on.
- Bake with the **lid on** at {{ovenOn}}°C.
- Remove the lid, lower the oven to **{{ovenLidOff}}°C** and bake on until deep golden brown. How long depends on the loaf:

- Optional: for the final 5 minutes, take the loaf out of the pot and set it directly on the oven rack for a crisper base.

Be careful: the pot and lid are extremely hot. Use thick oven gloves and have a heat-safe surface ready.

## Open bake (steel/stone with steam)

- Preheat a baking steel or stone for 45+ minutes at 240-250°C.
- Add steam: a tray of boiling water on a lower shelf, ice cubes in a hot tray, or wet towels in a tray. Be careful of steam burns and never pour cold water on glass.
- Bake with steam for the first 15-20 minutes, remove the steam source, then continue to finish.

## Scoring

- Scoring controls where the loaf expands.
- Use a very sharp blade or lame at a 30-45° angle for an "ear" (raised lip), or 90° for a flat cut.
- One confident, curved slash is better than hesitant sawing.
- Cold dough scores more cleanly.

## Doneness

- Deep golden-brown to dark mahogany crust; pale loaves taste flat.
- Internal temperature **96-99°C**.
- Hollow sound when tapped on the base.

## Cooling {steps=out,carve}

Wait at least {{coolingShort}} minutes (the app offers {{coolingShort}} or {{coolingLong}}), and an hour is better. The crumb is still cooking from residual heat and moisture redistributes as it cools.
