A quick feed, a warm dough and a proof on the counter, all in one day. It is the quickest way to a loaf, but it gives a less sour one, because much of the flavour develops during a long, cool ferment.

{{planSettings sameDay}}

## The plan

{{planFigure sameDay}}

Everything happens on the one day. The starter is fed at {{time sameDay feed}} and goes in at {{time sameDay starterIn}}. A warm dough (27°C) needs {{bulk 27}}, so it is shaped at {{time sameDay finalShape}} and proofs on the counter. The oven goes on at {{time sameDay ovenOn}}, the loaf goes in at {{time sameDay bake}} and it is ready to carve at {{time sameDay carve}}, {{fromFeed sameDay}} after the first feed.

## The feed

For one regular loaf, feed {{feedFor 2 1}} at {{time sameDay feed}}. A 1:2:2 is ready in {{feedWindow 2}}, so the starter goes in at {{time sameDay starterIn}}.

## What to check

- The dough temperature. This bake depends on a warm dough: at 27°C the bulk is {{bulk 27}}. A cooler kitchen means a longer bulk, which pushes the shaping and baking later into the evening ([[process#bulk|section 7, bulk fermentation]]).
- The counter proof. Check early, then poke the dough gently: it should spring back slowly. The times are in [[process#final-proof-no-fridge|Final proof without the fridge]], for example {{finalProof 22}} in a 22°C kitchen.
