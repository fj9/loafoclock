A quick feed, a warm dough and 2 to 3 hours of proofing on the counter, all in one day. It is the quickest way to a loaf, but it gives a less sour one, because the fridge is where most of the flavour develops.

## The plan

{{planFigure sameDay}}

Everything happens on the one day. The starter is fed at {{time sameDay feed}} and goes in at {{time sameDay starterIn}}. A warm dough (27°C) bulks for about 5 and a half hours, so it is shaped at {{time sameDay finalShape}} and proofs on the counter. The oven goes on at {{time sameDay ovenOn}}, the loaf goes in at {{time sameDay bake}} and it is ready to carve at {{time sameDay carve}}, {{fromFeed sameDay}} after the first feed.

## The feed

For one regular loaf, feed {{feedFor 2 1}} at {{time sameDay feed}}. A 1:2:2 is ready in {{feedWindow 2}}, so the starter goes in at 11.

## What to check

- **The dough temperature.** This bake depends on a warm dough: at 27°C the bulk is {{bulk 27}}. A cooler kitchen means a longer bulk, which pushes the shaping and baking later into the evening ([[process#bulk|section 6, step 5]]).
- **The counter proof.** Poke the dough gently: it should spring back slowly ([[process#step-8-final-proof|section 6, step 8]]).
