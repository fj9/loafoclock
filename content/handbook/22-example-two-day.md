The starter is fed in the morning, the dough is mixed in the afternoon and the whole of the first rise happens before bedtime. It goes in the fridge overnight and bakes the next morning. Nothing needs you in the night, and the dough is only in the fridge for the final proof.

## The plan

{{planFigure twoDay}}

**Day 1.** Feed the starter at {{time twoDay feed}}. The flour and water go together at {{time twoDay autolyse}}, and the starter goes in at {{time twoDay starterIn}}, when it is at its peak. The salt, honey and oil follow at {{time twoDay salt}}, then four folds, {{foldGap}} minutes apart. At 24°C the bulk takes about 7 hours, so the dough is shaped in the evening and goes into the fridge at {{time twoDay fridge}}.

**Day 2.** Turn the oven on at {{time twoDay ovenOn}} and bake straight from the fridge at {{time twoDay bake}}. The loaf is out at {{time twoDay out}}, and ready to carve an hour later, at {{time twoDay carve}}, {{fromFeed twoDay}} after the first feed.

## The feed

For one regular loaf, feed {{feedFor 2 1}}. A 1:2:2 is ready in {{feedWindow 2}}, which is why the starter is fed at breakfast and goes in after lunch. It makes enough for the dough and {{keepBack}} g to keep back as your next starter.

## What to check

- **The starter** is domed, bubbly and about double when it goes in (see [[starter#is-it-ready-to-use-the-peak|section 3.6]]).
- **The bulk** is finished when the dough has risen by the target for its temperature, not when the clock says so ([[process#bulk|section 6, step 5]]). Mark the level on a straight-sided tub.
- **The fridge** time is flexible: anything from {{fridgeMin}} to {{fridgeMax}} hours works, so the bake can move to suit you.
