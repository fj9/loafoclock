A bake can fit around sleep and work in several ways. Each of these has a full worked example in Part C, drawn as the app draws a plan, with every step that needs you inside waking hours (6:00 am to 11:00 pm). The days are called Day 1, Day 2 and so on, because the bake can start on any day.

## Which timeline suits you

- **Two days, everything in one day** ([[example-two-day|section C2]]). Feed at breakfast, shape before bed, bake from the fridge the next morning. The dough is only in the fridge for the final proof.
- **Three days, overnight levain** ([[example-overnight-levain|section C1]]). Feed in the evening, mix first thing the next morning, shape in the afternoon and bake from the fridge on the third day.
- **Same day, no fridge** ([[example-same-day|section C3]]). The quickest, with a warm dough and a counter proof. A less sour loaf.
- **After work, bake the next evening** ([[example-after-work|section C4]]). Feed before you leave, mix after you are home, and bake the next evening. The most "life-friendly" approach.
- **Two loaves, 24 hours apart** ([[example-two-loaves|section C5]]). One batch, two loaves, one baked after 12 hours in the fridge and one after 36.

[[example-temperature|Section C6]] says how a warmer or cooler dough or kitchen moves any of them.

## Working backwards

To plan any bake:

- Decide when you want to **eat** or **bake**.
- Subtract the bake: {{preheat}} minutes to preheat the oven, then the bake itself (see [[baking]]).
- Subtract the final proof (2 to 3 hours on the counter, {{fridgeMin}} to {{fridgeMax}} hours in the fridge).
- Subtract the pre-shape and shape (about 45 minutes).
- Subtract the bulk, counted from when the starter goes in (see [[process#bulk|section 6, step 5]]).
- Subtract the fermentolyse and the autolyse before it ({{restMin}} to {{restMax}} minutes each).
- Subtract the starter build, so that the starter is ready when it goes in (see [[starter#feeding-and-ratios|section 3.3]]).

[[example-after-work#working-backwards|A worked example of working backwards]] follows these steps with real times.
