A bake can fit around sleep and work in several ways. Each of these has a full worked example in Part C, drawn as the app draws a plan, with every step that needs you inside waking hours (6 am to 11 pm). The days are called Day 1, Day 2 and so on, because the bake can start on any day.

## Which timeline suits you

If this is your first loaf, start with the three-day timeline ([[example-overnight-levain|section C1]]). It fits a weekend well: feed on the evening before your first free day, mix and shape on that day, and bake on the morning of the next. Every other timeline uses the same steps with different timings.

- Three days, overnight feed ([[example-overnight-levain|section C1]]). Feed in the evening, mix first thing the next morning, shape in the afternoon and bake from the fridge on the third day.
- Two days, all work on Day 1, bake Day 2 ([[example-two-day|section C2]]). Feed at breakfast, shape before bed, bake from the fridge the next morning. The dough is only in the fridge for the final proof.
- All in a day, low sour ([[example-same-day|section C3]]). The quickest, with a warm dough and a counter proof. A less sour loaf.
- After work, bake the next evening ([[example-after-work|section C4]]). Feed before you leave, mix in the afternoon, and bake the next evening. The most "life-friendly" approach.
- Two loaves, 24 hours apart ([[example-two-loaves|section C5]]). The overnight feed timeline with one batch, two loaves, one baked after {{fridgeHours twoLoavesFirst}} hours in the fridge and one after {{fridgeHours twoLoavesSecond}}.

[[example-temperature|Section C6]] says how a warmer or cooler dough or kitchen moves any of them.

## Working backwards

To plan any bake:

- Decide when you want to eat or bake.
- Subtract the bake: {{preheat}} minutes to preheat the oven, then the bake itself (see [[baking]]).
- Subtract the final proof ({{fridgeMin}} to {{fridgeMax}} hours in the fridge, or the time in [[process#final-proof-no-fridge|the final proof table]] on the counter).
- Subtract the pre-shape and shape (about 45 minutes).
- Subtract the bulk, counted from when the starter goes in (see [[process#bulk|section 6, bulk fermentation]]). It already includes the fermentolyse, the salt rest and the folds.
- Subtract the autolyse before it ({{restMin}} to {{restMax}} minutes).
- Subtract the starter build, so that the starter is ready when it goes in (see [[starter#feeding-and-ratios|section 3.3]]).

[[example-after-work#working-backwards|A worked example of working backwards]] follows these steps with real times.
