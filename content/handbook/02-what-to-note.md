Most sourdough problems come down to three questions (see [[troubleshooting|section 12]]): was the starter ripe (risen to about double, with a domed top) when it was used, was the bulk fermentation (the first rise) long enough, judged by the dough rather than the clock, and was the dough temperature what you thought it was. Noting down the facts below lets you answer those questions afterwards. A short log of every bake, even a line or two, is the fastest way to learn: after a few loaves the patterns in your own kitchen start to show.

| What to note | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Kitchen temperature | It sets the pace of every stage. |
| When you fed the starter, and the feed (for example 20 g starter, 100 g flour, 100 g water) | It decides when the starter peaks. |
| How the starter looked when you used it (risen? domed? bubbly?) | A ripe starter is the foundation of the whole bake. |
| Water temperature and dough temperature just after mixing | Dough temperature decides how long bulk fermentation takes. |
| Time bulk started and how much the dough has risen (mark the tub with an elastic band or tape) | Bulk is finished by how much the dough has risen, not by time alone. |
| Flour type and the weights in your recipe | Different flours and hydrations behave differently. |
| Final proof: room or fridge, how long, and the poke test result | Overproofed and underproofed dough bake differently. |
| Oven temperature, bake time, and how the crust and crumb turned out | Helps pin down what to change next time. |

An example of a good note

> Kitchen about 20°C. Fed the starter at 8 am with 20 g starter, 100 g flour and 100 g water, and used it at 4 pm: it had risen but wasn't domed. Dough 22°C after mixing, bulk 5 hours without marking how far it rose. Shaped, fridge overnight, baked at 230°C for 40 minutes. The loaf came out flat and gummy.

The app's Log already keeps the feed, the dough temperature and the fridge time for each bake. Add the rest in the notes when you save it, and use the troubleshooting guide to work out what to change next time.
