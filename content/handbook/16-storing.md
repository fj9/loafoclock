## Storing

- Room temperature: cut side down on a board, or in a linen bag, bread bin or paper bag. Good for 2 to 4 days.
- Avoid the fridge: it makes bread stale faster. The exception is very hot, humid weather, when mould is the bigger risk.
- Plastic bag: it keeps the crumb soft for longer but makes the crust soft too. For a crisp crust, leave the loaf uncovered on day 1, then bag it from day 2 if the crumb starts to dry.

Sourdough tends to keep better than yeasted bread because of its acidity.

## Freezing

- Slice before freezing so you can toast slices straight from the freezer.
- Wrap tightly or use freezer bags. Keeps well for up to 3 months.
- Whole loaves can be frozen and reheated.

## Reviving stale bread

- Splash the crust lightly with water and bake at about 180°C for 5 to 10 minutes to refresh.
- Stale bread makes excellent toast, croutons, breadcrumbs, panzanella and French toast.
