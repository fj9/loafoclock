## Inclusions

- **Seeds** (sunflower, pumpkin, sesame, linseed): soak first or add dry, with a little extra water.
- **Olives, sun-dried tomatoes, herbs, cheese:** add during the laminating fold or later folds; pat wet items dry.
- **Dried fruit and nuts** (raisins, walnuts, cranberries): add at the second or third fold. Soaking dried fruit briefly stops it stealing water.
- **Cinnamon-raisin, jalapeño-cheddar, rosemary-garlic** are popular combinations.
- Add inclusions after the dough has some strength, around 10-20% of flour weight for most mix-ins.

## Enriched sourdough

Add butter, eggs, milk or sugar for brioche, buns or soft sandwich loaves. Fat and sugar slow the ferment slightly; allow a longer rise and consider a bit more starter.

## Other bakes with sourdough

- **Pizza dough:** 60-70% hydration, cold ferment 24-72 hours.
- **Focaccia:** very high hydration (80%+), olive oil, dimpled in a tray.
- **Bagels:** stiff dough, long cold retard, boil then bake.
- **Baguettes and ciabatta:** higher skill; more shaping and steam control.
- **English muffins, crumpets, flatbreads:** quick, griddle cooked.
- **Sandwich loaf in a tin:** easier to handle, even crumb.

## Using discard

- Pancakes and waffles
- Crackers
- Flatbreads and tortillas
- Muffins, cakes and brownies
- Pizza dough base (adds flavour, but note discard is acidic and has less leavening power)
