    About Loaf O'Clock

    It's always Loaf O'Clock somewhere.

    Hi, I'm Frankie, (fj9 on GitHub). Loaf O'Clock is a free sourdough planner that tells you what to do, and when.

    Why I made it

    Sourdough is mostly waiting, and the hard part is the timing: when to feed your starter so it's ready when you are, when to start, when you need to be in the house, and when you're free to get on with your day.

    My recipe began on a sourdough tea towel. Over time I added what I needed: timers, a way to work out the feed, amounts for one or two loaves, and a log of how each bake turned out.

    Then I wanted my mum to be able to use it too. So the words are plain, the tricky terms have a little hint, and the plan tells you when to be in the house.

    Who it's for

    Home bakers who make sourdough regularly and would like the timing taken care of. People new to sourdough who find the jargon off-putting. Anyone who has ever set an alarm for the middle of the night to feed a starter.

    It follows one recipe: a white-flour loaf with a little honey and olive oil, baked from cold in a Dutch oven. If that's close to how you bake, it should feel familiar. If not, the planning may still help.

    What it does

    • Guides you through ten steps, from feeding the starter to the oven, with timers.
    • Works backwards from when you want to start, to tell you when to feed and how much.
    • Shows a timeline of the whole bake, and how long until the next step.
    • Scales for one or two loaves, regular or large, at the hydration you choose.
    • Saves your place, so a locked phone or a reload doesn't lose your bake.
    • Keeps a log of your bakes, and works offline once you've opened it.

    How much to trust the times

    They're estimates. Starter rise times assume a kitchen around 21 to 22°C, and the bulk fermentation times use the dough temperature you give. A warm kitchen is faster and a cool one slower. Use the plan as a guide, and trust how the dough looks and feels.

    Your data

    Everything you log, and your bake in progress, stays on your device. There's no account, no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere. Use Back up in Settings if you want to keep a copy of your log or move it to another device.
